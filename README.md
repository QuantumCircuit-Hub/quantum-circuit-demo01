# QuantumCircuit Hub (QCH)

**An open platform for quantum circuit storage, versioning, search, optimization, benchmarking, and reproducible research.**

QuantumCircuit Hub (QCH) is an open-source research platform for managing, analyzing, searching, sharing, and optimizing quantum circuits.

Our long-term goal is to build an open infrastructure and community for quantum circuit data management and research.

## Vision

Quantum circuits are increasingly becoming important research artifacts. However, they are often scattered across repositories, benchmarks, frameworks, and publications.

QCH aims to provide a unified platform for:

- Quantum circuit storage and management
- Circuit metadata and provenance
- Circuit versioning and evolution
- Exact and approximate circuit search
- Circuit equivalence and similarity
- Circuit optimization and rewriting
- Benchmark collection and evaluation
- Reproducible quantum computing research

## Planned Components

- **QCH-Data** — circuit datasets and metadata
- **QCH-Core** — core data model and APIs
- **QCH-Search** — circuit search and similarity
- **QCH-Opt** — circuit optimization and rewriting
- **QCH-Bench** — benchmarks and evaluation

## Status

🚧 QuantumCircuit Hub is currently in its early development stage.

We welcome researchers, developers, and students interested in quantum circuits, databases, compilers, and quantum software engineering.
